About ZED-FITS

Founded in 2020 in Lusaka, ZED-FITS was created to celebrate Zambian design on the global stage. Our mission is to combine cultural heritage with contemporary style — offering collections that are bold, inclusive, and sustainably made.

Every piece tells a story of craftsmanship, identity, and modern expression.
