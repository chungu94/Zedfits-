About ZED-FiTS - Lusaka's Finest

Crafting Zambian Fashion • Rooted in Culture • Built for the Streets

OUR HERITAGE, REDEFINED

ZED-FiTS is Lusaka's finest premium fashion destination, blending Zambia's rich cultural heritage with contemporary streetwear style. We celebrate Chitenge — the vibrant, patterned fabric that tells stories of tradition, identity, and resilience — reimagined for the modern generation. From bold Chitenge streetwear to curated sneakers, caps, and designer glasses, we bring you apparel that honors Zambian culture while keeping you on trend.

📖

Our Story

Born in the heart of Lusaka in 2020, ZED-FiTS began as a small collective celebrating Zambian creatives.

🎯

Our Mission

To elevate Zambian fashion globally by merging Chitenge culture with contemporary streetwear.
